Letters to owners

One letter per page, addressed to "The Owner". Your logo and contact details come from Report options on any listing's Details page.

Before you send: writing to owners in the UK (not legal advice)
  • PropSnap writes to "The Owner", never to a named person. If you add names, for example from a Land Registry title, data protection law (UK GDPR) applies to how you get and use them.
  • Say who you are and how to reach you. Every starting letter tells the owner how to ask for no more letters.
  • If someone asks you to stop, stop. Give the home the "Do not contact" label and PropSnap leaves it out of every letter.
  • Be honest: do not claim a buyer, cash, a price or a deadline you do not have. If the home is with an estate agent, the owner's contract with that agent may still apply to a sale to you.
  • The Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) explains the rules on direct marketing.
